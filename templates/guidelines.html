Process

  1. Read Article A's headline, subheading and first paragraph.
  2. Note the highlighted link text — the phrase in Article A that links to Article B.
  3. Read Article B.
  4. Choose the one label that best describes the relationship. Your answer saves immediately and the next pair opens.
  5. Not sure? Choose your best judgement and optionally add a note explaining why.

Tips

  • Same Story means B is an earlier stage of the same event or ongoing development — it tells you what happened before A.
  • If B is an analysis or explainer about the same event, choose Related.
  • Sharing a person, place or topic alone makes a pair Related, not Same Story.
  • Some articles have no first paragraph; judge from the headline and subheading.
  • You can revisit and change any answer from the Progress page.
